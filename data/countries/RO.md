# Country pack: Romania (RO)

Used by `artemis offer`. Rules change: check the date below and the sources before relying on a
number, and have an accountant confirm anything that decides an offer.

Last reviewed: 2026-10 · Sources: Codul Fiscal (Legea 227/2015), Codul Muncii (Legea 53/2003).

## Employment contract (CIM): gross → net
- Employee contributions: **CAS (pension) 25%**, **CASS (health) 10%**, **income tax 10%** on
  gross minus CAS and CASS.
- Rule of thumb at senior salaries (no personal deduction): **net ≈ 58.5% of gross**. Check it
  against a real payslip.
- Some sectors and exemptions have changed since 2023 (the IT income-tax exemption is capped or
  removed). Don't assume it applies.

## Contractor alternatives (foreign employers often pay this way)
- **PFA** (sole trader) or **SRL** (micro-company) on a B2B contract. Net depends on the regime
  and the year's thresholds, so compute it with current rates and say so.
- Price in what a contractor loses: paid leave (≥20 working days on a CIM), paid sick leave, the
  pension pillar II contribution, unemployment cover, and notice protection.

## Employment-law points for offers
- **Probation:** up to 90 calendar days for execution roles and **120 days for management roles**.
- **Notice when resigning:** at most 20 working days for execution roles and at most 45 working
  days for management roles (art. 81), whatever the contract says. When the employer dismisses: at
  least 20 working days.
- **Post-termination non-compete** (Codul Muncii art. 21–24): valid only if the contract sets a
  monthly indemnity (at least 50% of the average gross pay of the last 6 months), a duration of at
  most 2 years, and a defined area and activity. Worth negotiating, or getting reviewed by a lawyer.
