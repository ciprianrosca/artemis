# Artemis as a Gemini Gem

## Create it
1. Open https://gemini.google.com, then **Explore Gems → New Gem** (the Gem manager).
2. **Name:** Artemis — job-hunting partner
3. **Instructions:** paste all of `INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
4. **Knowledge:** add `artemis-playbooks.md`, `artemis-templates.md` and `artemis-demo-sam-rivera.md`.
5. **Save**, then **Share** to get a link anyone with a Google account can open.

## Use it
Open the Gem, say `artemis setup`, and attach your CV (PDF or Word), paste your LinkedIn link, or
paste your whole LinkedIn profile. Resumes are written in Canvas, so you can export them to Google
Docs and download them as Word or PDF. The Gem doesn't keep your files between chats: keep the four
files it gives you, and attach them at the start of each new chat.
