# Artemis in the Claude app (claude.ai, desktop, mobile)

## Set it up once
1. **Skills:** open Settings → Capabilities, find Skills, and upload each zip from `skills/`
   (six of them). Code execution must be on for skills to work.
2. **Project:** create a Project called "Artemis" and paste all of `PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` into
   its instructions.
3. **Optional demo:** add `artemis-demo-sam-rivera.md` to the Project's knowledge.

## Use it
In the Project, say `artemis setup` and attach your CV (PDF or Word), paste your LinkedIn link, or
paste your whole LinkedIn profile. Artemis gives you `master_cv.md`, `knowledge.json`,
`contact.json` and `pipeline.md`: add them to the Project's knowledge. When Artemis gives you an
updated version later, replace the old file.
